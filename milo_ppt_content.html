Slide 03 Solution What is Milo
Milo is an AI-powered smart toy. It listens, understands, and responds — like a trusted friend.
What Milo does in one line
"A stuffed toy powered by AI that knows your child, comforts them in your voice, and keeps you informed — all day, every day."

Slide 04 Features 4 Core Features
Four features. One toy. Built for real family life.
01 · Cry Alert
Detects crying using on-device audio AI. Plays a parent comfort clip instantly. Sends a push notification to the parent's phone with time and duration.
02 · Sentiment-Adaptive Storytelling
Analyses the child's voice tone in real time. Responds with humor, stories, and warmth matched to the child's current emotional state — never boring, never overwhelming.
03 · Parent Voice Layer
Parents record 20 phrases in the app. Milo plays them at emotionally matched moments — comfort when the child is sad, praise when they do well, lullabies at bedtime.

Full voice-based conversation powered by Groq AI. Child asks anything — space, animals, why the sky is blue — and Milo answers in a warm, child-safe, curiosity-first way.

Slide 05 Feature Deep Dive Cry Alert
Feature 01: Cry Alert — real-time safety for parents who can't always be there.
How it works
  • Microphone is always on in a low-power background thread
  • YAMNet audio classifier checks for cry signals every second
  • Cry confirmed after 3 seconds above confidence threshold
  • Comfort clip plays instantly — zero cloud latency
  • Push notification fires to parent's phone in under 5 seconds
What the parent sees
  • Notification: "Milo detected crying at 3:14 PM"
  • Duration: "lasted approximately 45 seconds"
  • Weekly pattern: "Priya cried 3 evenings this week around 6 PM"
  • Parent can listen to comfort clip that played
Why this matters

A working parent in a meeting gets a notification. They know their child is being comforted by their own voice. That is peace of mind no other product currently offers.

Speaker notes
Trigger the live cry demo here if you're doing it. Play a 5-second crying audio file near the mic. Show the notification arriving on the phone. Let the silence do the work after it fires.
Slide 06 Feature Deep Dive Sentiment + Storytelling
Feature 02: Milo doesn't just hear what you say. It hears how you feel.
The sentiment pipeline
  • Child's voice is captured → librosa analyses pitch, pace, and volume variance in under 100ms
  • Maps to one of 4 mood states: sad · tired · excited · calm
  • Mood signal is passed into the AI system prompt before generating a response
  • AI adjusts: tone, vocabulary, story energy, humor level, and response length — automatically
Example — same question, different moods
Child says Mood detected How Milo responds
"Tell me about dinosaurs" Excited Fast, playful, full of fun facts and silly comparisons — "T-Rex had arms shorter than yours!"
"Tell me about dinosaurs" Sad / tired Slow, warm, story-mode — "Once upon a time, a gentle dinosaur named Pip…"
"I don't want to sleep" Tired Soft voice, breathing exercise disguised as a story, transitions to lullaby clip
Speaker notes
This is the feature that makes judges lean forward. The key line: "Every other voice assistant gives the same answer every time. Milo gives the right answer for right now." Let that land before moving on.
Slide 07 Feature Deep Dive Parent Voice Layer
Feature 03: When a child hears "I love you baby" from Milo — it's actually their parent's voice.
How parents set it up
  • Open the Milo parent app → go to Voice Studio
  • Record 20 phrases across 5 categories
  • Comfort: "It's okay baby, I'm here"
  • Praise: "I'm so proud of you!"
  • Bedtime: "Sleep tight, I love you to the moon"
  • Greeting: "Good morning sunshine!"
  • Gentle guidance: "Let's try again, you've got this"
How Milo uses them
  • Cry detected → comfort clip plays within 1 second
  • Child answers a game correctly → praise clip plays
  • Child says "goodnight" → bedtime clip + lullaby
  • Child wakes the toy → greeting clip plays first
  • No matching clip? Milo responds in warm neutral tone
The key insight

We're not cloning the parent's voice. We're giving the parent a presence. The child hears their actual parent — not a robot pretending to be them.

Speaker notes
This is your emotional peak. Ideally, do the live recording moment here — invite a judge or teammate to record "I love you, you're doing amazing" into the app. Then trigger Milo to play it. Watch the room react.
Slide 08 Tech Stack How We Built It
Built in 15 days. Everything is open-source or free-tier.
Groq + Llama 3
AI brain. Generates all responses. Sub-500ms latency.
Whisper STT
Speech-to-text. Converts child speech to text locally.
YAMNet
Google audio classifier. Detects crying in real time.
librosa
Audio analysis. Extracts mood from voice tone and pace.
Python + Flask
Core logic. Voice loop, threading, clip playback, API calls.
Firebase
Real-time database + push notifications to parent phone.
React + Vite
Parent app. Dashboard, cry alerts, voice recording studio.
pyttsx3 / gTTS
Text-to-speech. Speaks Milo's responses to the child.
Privacy architecture
  • No raw audio ever stored or sent to cloud — only processed metadata (mood tag, cry event)
  • Parent voice clips encrypted and stored locally on device
  • All AI inference happens on-device or via encrypted API call
Speaker notes
Keep this slide fast — 45 seconds max. The judges care about "did it work" more than "how did you build it." End with: "The entire stack costs under ₹6,000 in hardware and zero in software."
